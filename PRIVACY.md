# Tinyfolk privacy policy

Tinyfolk does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- **Camera.** If you turn on "Follow my eyes", the front camera is used to find where faces are, so the scene can be drawn from your point of view. Each frame is analysed on your device by Apple's Vision framework and discarded immediately. No image, video, face data or measurement is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time; the app works without it.
- Music and sounds are synthesized on your device.
- Screen recordings (Tinyfolk Pro) are made with Apple's ReplayKit. They stay on your device and are only shared if you save or share them yourself. The microphone is not used.
- Your choices (character, stage, music) are stored on your device.
- Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Tinyfolk only receives whether Tinyfolk Pro is unlocked.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/tinyfolk/issues
